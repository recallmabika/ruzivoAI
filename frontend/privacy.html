  • Hurukuro dzedzidzo: Mibvunzo, maekisesaizi, pamwe netsumo dzaunobvunza kuti zvirambe zviri mudura rako rehurukuro paunodzidza.
  • Kudzivirira Mabhot (Security & Bot Defense): Kero ye IP yenguva pfupi pamwe nekushandiswa kweGoogle reCAPTCHA Enterprise kudzivirira kupaza sisitimu (brute-force attacks).

2. Kushandiswa kweRuzivo rweGoogle API (Google User Data)

Ruzivo AI inoshandisa Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0) kurerutsira vanoda kupinda neGoogle:

  • Kutevedzera Mitemo yeGoogle: Kushandiswa kwese kweruzivo runobva kuGoogle kunotevedzera zvizere Google API Services User Data Policy, kusanganisira mitemo ye Limited Use requirements.

3. Chengetedzo yeRuzivo (Security & Encryption)

Isu tinoshandisa nzira dzechimanjemanje dzekuchengetedza data:

  • Dandemutande rakavharwa: Zvose zvinofamba pakati pekombiyuta yako ne server zvinodzivirirwa ne SSL/TLS (HTTPS) encryption.
  • CSRF & Brute-Force: Sisitimu inozvidzivirira yoga panzira dzekunyepedzera kana kuedza mapasiwedhi akawandisa.

Welcome to Ruzivo AI ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at https://www.ruzivo-ai.tech and https://ruzivo-ai.tech. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you interact with our educational platform.

Key Commitment: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks. Your data is used exclusively to deliver and enhance our educational Shona AI service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you register an account, authenticate, or use our services:

  • Account Information: When registering, we collect your name or nickname, email address, and encrypted credentials. If you choose to sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your verified email address, name, and profile picture URL as authorized by your Google account.
  • Educational Interactions: Questions, educational prompts, Shona language exercises, and conversations you engage in with Ruzivo AI to maintain your study history and facilitate learning continuity.
  • Security & Bot Defense: IP addresses and verification tokens via Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to detect automated attacks, abuse, and brute-force intrusion attempts.

2. Google User Data Policy Compliance

Ruzivo AI integrates Google Identity Services for user authentication. In compliance with Google's requirements:

  • We only access basic profile information (email address, name, profile picture) required for account creation and session authentication.
  • We do not request access to Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or sensitive non-profile scopes.
  • Ruzivo AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Security and Encryption

We implement defense-in-depth technical security controls:

  • Cryptographic Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. They are salted and hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 over thousands of iterations.
  • Transport Layer Security: All data in transit between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS).
  • Session Integrity: Authentication utilizes cryptographically signed session tokens and strict CSRF tokens to safeguard each interaction.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your educational and personal data:

  • Access and Export: You can review your profile, learning history, and saved memories directly within platform settings.
  • Permanent Deletion: You have the right to request full deletion of your account and all associated conversation history at any time. This can be executed through our verified self-service 2FA account deletion workflow.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use essential cookies and browser storage strictly required for platform operation (session continuity, CSRF defense, and interface theme preferences). We do not deploy third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
