Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026 • Effective date: October 27, 2026

Welcome to Ruzivo AI ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at https://www.ruzivo-ai.tech and https://ruzivo-ai.tech. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you interact with our educational platform.

Key Commitment: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks. Your data is used exclusively to deliver and enhance our educational Shona AI service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you register an account, authenticate, or use our services:

  • Account Information: When registering, we collect your name or nickname, email address, and encrypted credentials. If you choose to sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your verified email address, name, and profile picture URL as authorized by your Google account.
  • Educational Interactions: Questions, educational prompts, Shona language exercises, and conversations you engage in with Ruzivo AI to maintain your study history and facilitate learning continuity.
  • Technical & Log Data: Standard HTTP request metadata including IP addresses (used strictly for brute-force rate-limiting and security defense), browser type, and CSRF token session state.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Ruzivo AI Shona language learning assistant.
  • To authenticate user sessions securely and maintain account integrity.
  • To prevent fraudulent access, automated attacks (such as brute-force or SQL injection attempts), and abusive traffic.
  • To continuously improve Shona natural language processing accuracy and curriculum relevance based on user feedback (upvotes/downvotes).
  • To respond to your inquiries submitted to support@ruzivo-ai.tech.

3. Password Security & Cryptography

We employ state-of-the-art cryptographic safeguards. User passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are hashed using PBKDF2 with HMAC-SHA256 with a unique, cryptographically secure 16-byte salt per user across 100,000 iterations. No employee, administrator, or third party has access to your raw password.

4. Google API User Data & OAuth Compliance

Ruzivo AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We only request access to basic profile information (`openid`, `profile`, `email`) for authentication and profile identification.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising purposes.

5. Your Rights & Data Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Access & Export: You can view your account profile and chat history anytime within the platform settings.
  • Permanent Deletion: You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated conversations at any time through the platform settings or by submitting a request to support@ruzivo-ai.tech. Upon confirmation, your account record and conversations are permanently deleted from our PostgreSQL database with zero lingering retention.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection team:

  • Email: support@ruzivo-ai.tech
  • Website: https://www.ruzivo-ai.tech
  • Location: Harare, Zimbabwe
