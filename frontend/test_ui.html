Kuchengetedzwa kweChakavanzika (Privacy Policy)
Chitsidzo cheRuzivo: Ruzivo AI runokoshesa zvakadzama kuchengetedzwa kweruzivo rwenyu rwakavanzika. Hatitengese kana kupa ruzivo rwenyu kune vekunze (third parties).

1. Ruzivo Rwatinochera (Data We Collect)

Patinoita zvidzidzo, tinochengetedza email yenyu, zita renyu remadunhurirwa kana zita ramakanyoresa naro, pamwe chete nehurukuro dzedzidzo dzamunoita naRuzivo kuti mukwanise kudzokorora kudzidza pava paya.

2. Kuchengetedzwa kwePasiwedhi (Security & Cryptography)

Mapasiwedhi ose anochengetedzwa zvakadzama pachishandiswa unyanzvi hwePBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 ine munyu (salt) yakasiyana kumushandisi wega wega. Hapana mushandi weRuzivo anokwanisa kuona pasiwedhi yenyu.

3. Dzidzo neKuvandudza AI (Model Training & Improvement)

Hurukuro dzenyu dzinobatsira kusimudzira mutauro weChiShona muAI kuburikidza neFeedback Mechanism chete kana maipa mvumo (upvote/downvote). Ruzivo harushandiswi kushambidza zvinhu zvekutengesa (no marketing tracking).

4. Kodzero Yenyu yeKudzima Zvose (Right to Erasure)

Mune kodzero yakazara yekudzima hurukuro dzenyu chero nguva, kana kudzima akaundi yenyu zvachose (Delete Account) kubva mudatabase yeRuzivo pasina kusiya kana chimwe chezvinyorwa zvenyu.
